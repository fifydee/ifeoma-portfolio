Analyst's working principles

The production analyst will answer from the selected companies' filings and cited evidence — not from an ungrounded general response.

  • Show source references beside material claims.
  • Separate reported facts from derived metrics.
  • Call out missing or conflicting disclosures.
  • Explain uncertainty and reporting-period differences.
  • Decline to invent figures where evidence is absent.
