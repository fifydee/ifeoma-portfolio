A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curious by nature.
A builder by practice.

I'm Ifeoma, an AI Creative Technologist interested in what happens when creativity, technology and human curiosity come together.

My journey spans computing, secondary research analysis and project management. Today, I bring those experiences into my work with AI, creating visual stories and building digital tools that make ideas easier to understand and use.

I enjoy exploring how things work, asking questions and learning across different fields. That curiosity shapes the way I approach every project, whether I'm developing a video concept, building a prototype or experimenting with a new tool.

I believe the best work often starts with a good question and the willingness to explore what is possible.

I build, I experiment, I learn, and I share the journey.
